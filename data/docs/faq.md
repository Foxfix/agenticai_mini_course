# BrightCart FAQ

**Do you price match?** Yes. We match the price of the same new item from a major US retailer within 14 days of purchase. Marketplace sellers don't count.

**Can I cancel an order?** Yes, while the order is "processing". After that, return it instead.

**Do you sell refurbished products?** Yes, in the "Renewed" section. Renewed products have a 6-month warranty.

**How do I contact a human?** Ask the assistant to "talk to a person". A support agent replies by email within 1 business day, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Eastern Time.

**Do you store my card number?** No. Payments are handled by our payment provider. Support will never ask for your full card number.
