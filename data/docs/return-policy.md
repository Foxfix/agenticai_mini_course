# BrightCart return policy

## Standard returns
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging and include all accessories, cables and manuals.

## Opened electronics
Opened headphones, earbuds and smart watches can be returned within 15 days of delivery. For hygiene reasons, in-ear products must have their original ear tips sealed in the bag.

## Items we can't take back
Gift cards, downloadable software and items damaged by the customer can't be returned.

## Late orders
If an order arrives more than 2 days after the promised date, the customer gets a 10% discount on the next order. The discount code is LATE10. It is valid for 60 days.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Shipping costs are refunded only if the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## How to start a return
Customers start a return from "My orders" on the website, or by asking the support assistant. Every return gets a prepaid shipping label by email.
