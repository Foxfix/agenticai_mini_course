# BrightCart shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 9.99 USD. Orders over 50 USD ship for free with standard delivery.

## Order status
Every order has one of these statuses: processing, shipped, delayed, delivered. "Delayed" means the carrier reported a problem and the delivery date moved. Customers get an email every time the status changes.

## Changing the address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order is "processing". After an order is shipped, the address can't be changed, but the customer can ask the carrier to hold the parcel.

## Countries
We ship to the United States and Canada. We don't ship to PO boxes.
