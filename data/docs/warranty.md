# BrightCart warranty

## Standard warranty
Every product sold by BrightCart has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty starts on the delivery date.

## BrightCare Plus
BrightCare Plus extends the warranty to 3 years and adds accidental damage protection (one claim per year). It costs 10% of the product price and can be added within 30 days of delivery.

## What is not covered
Normal wear, batteries that lose less than 20% of their capacity in the first year, and damage from unofficial repairs are not covered.

## How to make a claim
Customers send a short description and a photo of the problem. Support replies within 1 business day with either a replacement, a repair or a refund.
